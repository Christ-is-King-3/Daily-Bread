Christ Healing the Blind

El Greco (Domenikos Theotokopoulos) · ca. 1570

El Greco brings the healing of a blind man into a crowded architectural setting. Watch the hands and the directions of the figures’ gazes: the scene gathers around an encounter of attention and trust.

A living inheritance

Many centuries. Many ways of seeing.

This collection brings together works from The Metropolitan Museum of Art and Cleveland Museum of Art. It includes medieval manuscript leaves, Bible woodcuts, icons, stained glass and works on paper, alongside paintings from later centuries. Some images depict biblical events; others reflect devotional traditions or artistic interpretations.

Each image is individually designated public domain or CC0 by its museum. Copies are held with Daily Bread so that the gallery does not rely on external image servers. Artwork is shown in full; the enlarged view lets you explore the details. Museum records preserve titles, dates, attribution qualifications and provenance.

Reflections are written for Daily Bread and labelled separately from historical information. Scripture links are included where a direct connection has been identified. Art from different Christian traditions is presented for thoughtful appreciation.

A new featured work is selected by your local calendar date, cycling through all 131 works before repeating while the collection remains unchanged. Use the date controls to explore other days. Met Open Access ↗ · Cleveland Open Access ↗
